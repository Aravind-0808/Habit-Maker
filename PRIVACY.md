# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Habit Maker is an open-source app. It is built to keep your data on your phone.

## What the app stores

The app stores the following **only on your device**:

- your habits, habit check-ins, focus sessions, routine and block rules
- your settings
- if you create an account: your name, your email and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself)

## What the app does not do

- It has no server and does not ask for the Internet permission, so it can't send your data anywhere.
- It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
- It does not sell or share any data.

## Special permissions

- **Accessibility service:** used only to see which app is on screen, and for some apps the page or the website address, so the app can show the block prompt you set up. The app doesn't record, store or send what is on your screen.
- **Usage access:** used to show your screen time, unlocks and app limits. It stays on your device.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen intent:** used for reminders, the focus timer and the wake-up alarm.

## Backups

- **Export** writes a JSON file to a location you choose. Keep it safe; it contains your app data.
- If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup. You control this in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Use **Profile → Delete all data**, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything the app stored.

## Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
